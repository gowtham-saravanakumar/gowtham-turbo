# Creative brief coverage

This rebuild is structured against the supplied 30-part cinematic portfolio brief.

1. **Opening cinematic** — blackout boot sequence, stars, signature ship wake-up, fly-in camera, 3D environmental identity typography.
2. **Spaceship guide** — one procedural Asterion-X ship persists through the entire journey with cockpit, wings, weapons, reactor and engine systems.
3. **Interactive camera** — cinematic intro orbit, chase, cockpit, wide establishing mode, dynamic FOV, shake, pointer offset and interpolation.
4. **Space flight** — one continuous scroll-driven route through a persistent 3D universe.
5. **Combat** — twin energy projectiles, drone targets, hit destruction and particle bursts.
6. **Battle transition** — asteroid field + warning UI + drones + portal corridor into the next destination.
7. **About planet** — AEON-01 with atmosphere shell, city arc and holographic personnel interface.
8. **Experience journey** — verified practice is framed as mission destinations rather than a conventional timeline.
9. **Projects as worlds** — project mission selector corresponds to destinations inside the journey.
10. **Game development lab** — running physics/simulation visualization instead of an icon grid.
11. **Full-stack environment** — 3D data spine, connected nodes and moving packets; only verified technologies are claimed.
12. **Code environment** — floating spatial code/data panels around the flight path.
13. **Skills constellation** — connected 3D star nodes plus accessible DOM selection/readout.
14. **Sci-fi HUD** — sector, coordinates, status, energy, targeting reticle and camera dock without covering the scene.
15. **Extreme parallax** — persistent depth layers from particles, worlds, ship, labels, UI and foreground vignette.
16. **Mouse interaction** — pointer affects ship banking, yaw/pitch and camera offset with damping.
17. **Mission navigation** — mission-control nav triggers smooth travel plus portal-flash transition.
18. **Boss moments** — intro fly-by, asteroid belt, drone engagement, portal, station reveal, orbital crown and final pullback.
19. **Signature scene** — massive multi-ring orbital crown representing the complete system.
20. **Final mission contact** — holographic command panel at Dock-09 with transmission feedback.
21. **Final cinematic** — camera pulls outward after the docking/final system sequence and resolves on Mission Complete.
22. **3D physics** — inertia, momentum-style interpolation, ship banking, acceleration feel, particles and camera shake.
23. **Cinematic effects** — bloom, depth of field, vignette, grain/noise, chromatic aberration, emissive lighting, fog and dynamic FOV.
24. **Sound-ready** — opt-in synthesized engine ambience; audio is OFF by default and never autoplays.
25. **Mobile** — responsive composition, reduced particle counts, hidden secondary HUD and reduced post-processing.
26. **Performance** — adaptive visual quality based on hardware and measured frame cadence; instanced asteroid field and frustum culling.
27. **UI/UX** — strong type hierarchy, readable information surfaces, high contrast and minimal controls.
28. **Accessibility** — semantic DOM portfolio remains available, keyboard controls/focus, skip link and reduced-motion mode.
29. **Original universe** — all spacecraft, worlds, HUD language and architecture are procedural/original; no franchise assets are used.
30. **Final statement** — the portfolio is implemented as a continuous 3D mission, not a normal site with a 3D hero added on top.
